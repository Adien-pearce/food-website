# food website
Transform your kitchen chaos into culinary creation.  AI Leftovers Chef is an intelligent web application designed to eliminate food waste and inspire your next meal. Leveraging cutting-edge AI and computer vision, this project revolutionizes the way you cook by generating personalized, delicious recipes based entirely on the food you already have.  Key Features Leftovers-to-Recipe AI: Input your available ingredients, or simply snap a photo of your fridge contents/leftovers using your camera, and our AI model generates unique, complete recipes.  Computer Vision Integration: Utilizes image recognition to automatically identify and list food items from uploaded photos, making ingredient tracking effortless and accurate.  Personalized Cooking: Recipes are tailored to minimize food waste and can be refined by dietary restrictions (e.g., vegan, keto), cuisine preference, and cooking time.  Detailed Recipe Output: Each generated recipe includes a creative title, ingredient list with suggested quantities, and step-by-step instructions.  Full-Stack Implementation: Built with a modern tech stack (e.g., Python/FastAPI for the backend, React/Next.js for the frontend, and integrating a robust AI model like OpenAI GPT or Gemini) suitable for rapid development and deployment.
